The Cause

Why Aliganj Deserves District Status

Aliganj has grown in population, ambition, and public need. A new district will bring decision-making closer to the people.

01

Administrative Distance

Current district headquarters are too far for everyday public grievances, land issues, and local service bottlenecks.

  • • Faster citizen access to officials
  • • Quicker handling of public complaints
  • • More timely local governance
02

Economic Growth

Becoming a district will unlock dedicated infrastructure investments for roads, markets, hospitals, and judicial access.

  • • New public infrastructure investment
  • • Better healthcare and civil facilities
  • • Stronger local commerce and investment
03

Youth & Employment

A newly formed district will create local job opportunities across administration, services, and public delivery systems.

  • • More local government jobs
  • • Faster public service delivery
  • • Empowered youth and entrepreneurship
